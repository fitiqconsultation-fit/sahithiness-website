Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information Sahithiness collects through sahithiness.in and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The health and lifestyle answers you give when you join (for example delivery details, PCOS / PCOD and other health conditions).
  • The Gmail ID you want the videos shared with.
  • Payment confirmation from Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How we use it

Only to deliver your program safely: to share your classes, send you confirmations and receipts, and contact you about your program. We do not sell or rent your information.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored securely in Google Workspace tools (Google Sheets, Calendar and Drive) that only Sahithi and her team can access. Payments are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. We keep records only as long as needed for your program and for legal and tax requirements.
