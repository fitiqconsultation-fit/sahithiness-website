Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Please read this before you pay. If anything is unclear, message us first and we will help you choose.

Monthly programs (₹2,784 a month)

  • Because videos are shared with you as soon as your payment is confirmed, a month that has started is not refundable.
  • You can stop at any time: simply do not renew for the next month. There are no automatic charges.
  • If you paid but did not receive your videos, or were charged twice, you get a full refund of that payment.
  • If your doctor advises you not to exercise before your videos are shared, message us and we will refund you in full.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

To request a refund or cancellation

WhatsApp or call +91 99801 84295 with your name and order ID from your confirmation email.
