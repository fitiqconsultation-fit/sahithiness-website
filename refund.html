Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Please read this before you pay. If anything is unclear, message us first and we will help you choose.

1:1 Consultation Call (₹999)

  • You can reschedule once, free, up to 12 hours before your call. Message us on WhatsApp.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your call for a full refund.
  • Calls missed without notice are not refunded.
  • If Sahithi has to cancel, you get a new time or a full refund, your choice.

General Yoga (recorded classes)

  • Because the classes are shared with you immediately after payment, this program is not refundable once access is given.
  • If you cannot open the videos because of a problem on our side and we cannot fix it within 48 hours, you get a full refund.

Prenatal and Postnatal programs

  • Full refund if you cancel before your first live session.
  • Full refund if, after reviewing your health details, Sahithi or your doctor advises that the program is not suitable for you.
  • After live sessions have started, fees are not refunded. For medical reasons, your remaining sessions can be paused and resumed later.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

To request a refund or cancellation

WhatsApp or call +91 99801 84295 with your name and order ID from your confirmation email.
